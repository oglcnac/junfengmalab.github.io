    <span class="research-eyebrow">Our Research</span>
    <h1>Protein O-GlcNAcylation in health and disease</h1>
    <p>Dr. Junfeng Ma’s research team studies proteins and their post-translational modifications in physiology and pathology, with a particular focus on O-linked β-N-acetylglucosamine (O-GlcNAc) modification, or O-GlcNAcylation.</p>
    <p>Our work combines analytical chemistry, proteomics, biochemistry, and molecular and cellular biology. We develop chromatographic and mass spectrometric methods for precision proteomics and investigate how protein O-GlcNAcylation contributes to cancer, diabetes, neurodegenerative disease, and other metabolic disorders.</p>
